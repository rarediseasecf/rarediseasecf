================================================================================
                               RareDiseaseCF
        Privacy-first, hospital-verified crowdfunding for rare disease care
================================================================================

ONE-LINE SUMMARY
----------------
RareDiseaseCF is a crowdfunding platform for rare disease treatment where every
campaign is verified by a real hospital, the funding goal is locked to the exact
treatment gap, donations sit in escrow, and money is released in milestones
directly to the care institution, never as unrestricted cash to an individual.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Rare disease treatment is expensive. Therapies, specialist drugs, and long
hospital stays often cost more than insurance, government schemes, and family
savings can cover. Families turn to crowdfunding, but today's crowdfunding has
four serious problems:

  1. Trust. Donors cannot tell whether a medical story is real. Fake or
     exaggerated campaigns are common, so genuine patients get less support.

  2. Wrong amounts. Campaigns usually ask for the full treatment cost, even when
     insurance or assistance already covers part of it. Donors have no way to
     see the real remaining gap.

  3. Misuse of funds. Money typically goes straight to a personal account, with
     no guarantee it is spent on the treatment it was raised for.

  4. Privacy. To prove they are real, patients are pressured to post diagnoses,
     prescriptions, and hospital bills publicly. That sensitive health
     information stays on the internet permanently.

In short: donors cannot trust, patients have to overshare, and nobody can see
where the money goes.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE SOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RareDiseaseCF fixes each problem with a specific mechanism:

  Trust        -> The patient's own hospital verifies the case through a secure,
                  single-use email link sent only to a whitelisted hospital
                  address. Verified campaigns show a badge such as
                  "Verified by billing@hospital.org".

  Right amount -> The funding gap is never typed in freely:
                      Funding gap = treatment cost
                                    - external assistance (insurance, grants)
                                    - patient's own contribution
                  Gap attested by the hospital, locked by the program.

  Fund safety  -> Donations are held in escrow and released in four 25%
                  tranches, each by a permissionless instruction the moment
                  its threshold is crossed. The money goes only to the
                  hospital wallet registered for the campaign, not to the
                  patient. During the Devnet beta the team still holds the
                  program's upgrade key.

  Privacy      -> Medical documents are encrypted and only the verifying
                  hospital can see them. With the patient's or guardian's
                  consent, the site shows the patient's name, condition,
                  treatment and funding gap. Donations and payouts, including
                  the hospital's receiving wallet, are public on Solana. No
                  health data is ever put on the blockchain.

  Transparency -> A public transparency page shows each campaign's life story
                  (submitted, verified, published, donations, milestones
                  settled) plus a donor leaderboard that respects donors who
                  chose to stay anonymous.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHO USES IT (ROLES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Patient / caregiver (creator)
     Submits a case with treatment details, costs, the hospital's email,
     2 to 8 supporting documents, and an optional public campaign photo.
     No crypto wallet is needed.

  2. Hospital verifier
     Receives a secure magic link by email, reviews the documents and numbers,
     then approves or rejects with a reason code. When approving, the hospital
     chooses how it will receive funds: Stablecoin (USDC on Solana), which asks
     for the institution's Solana wallet address, or Fiat (bank transfer),
     which is marked "coming soon" and cannot be selected for approval yet.

  3. Donor
     Browses verified campaigns and donates with USDC from a Solana wallet
     (Phantom or Solflare). Card / fiat donations are shown as "coming soon".
     Can donate anonymously.

  4. Platform admin
     Manages the hospital whitelist and publishes campaigns. Milestone
     payouts are sent by the server right after each donation (anyone may
     send them); the admin page only offers a manual retry.

  5. Provider (optional on-chain path)
     A registered care provider confirms fulfillment of treatment before the
     full payment is released on-chain.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. END-TO-END FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1  Submit
          The patient fills a 5-step form: treatment need, cost and institution,
          assistance, public details (patient name, condition, treatment and a
          required consent box), then an optional campaign photo and the
          documents. The photo is shown publicly, so the form warns not to use
          documents or anything identifying; location/camera data is stripped.
          The hospital email must be an
          institutional address; Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook and similar personal
          domains are rejected. The case gets a short 5-letter ID
          (for example K7MQP).

  Step 2  Hospital verification
          If the hospital's email domain is on the whitelist, the system sends a
          signed magic link to that address. The link is valid for at most
          24 hours, works only once, and is tied to that one case and that one
          email.

  Step 3  Approve and lock
          The hospital opens the link, views the documents (and a preview of
          the public campaign photo, if any), confirms the numbers, and picks
          a payout method. Choosing Stablecoin (USDC) asks
          for the institution's Solana wallet address, which is checked and
          stored encrypted; Fiat (bank transfer) shows a "not supported yet,
          rolling out soon" notice. On approval the funding gap is locked, the campaign
          is created with four LOCKED milestones and a 180-day window, and it
          is published with the verified badge. In on-chain
          mode the server also creates the campaign escrow on Solana, bound to
          the hospital's wallet. A case with a zero funding gap cannot be
          approved.

  Step 4  Donate
          Donors give in USDC (card / fiat donations are coming soon). Each
          donation is a real USDC transfer from the donor's wallet into the
          campaign's on-chain escrow vault. The server checks every donation on
          the blockchain (successful, right campaign vault, right token, exact
          amount) before counting it, and never counts the same transaction
          twice.

  Step 5  Milestone tranches
          Each 25% tranche is released by a permissionless instruction
          (settle_milestone) the moment each 25% threshold is crossed. The
          program checks the threshold itself and only pays the hospital's
          registered wallet, so anyone can send the instruction; the server
          sends it right after each donation and retries hourly if a send
          fails. The same applies at 50%, 75%, and 100%. Each
          payout is recorded as e.g. SETTLE-K7MQP-25 with its transaction
          signature, and the Milestone escrow section links to it on Solana
          Explorer. Paying the same milestone twice is blocked, and the total
          paid can never exceed what was raised or the goal.

  Step 6  Campaign ends (180-day rule)
          After 180 days, everything raised but not yet paid out goes to the
          hospital, so partial funding still reaches the treating hospital.
          Example: if 48% was raised, 25% was already paid at the milestone
          and the remaining 23% is sent now. The program enforces this
          (release_expired_funds): anyone can send it, but the money can only
          go to the hospital's wallet. The server sends it in an hourly sweep,
          and the Milestone escrow section shows it with a Solana Explorer
          link.

  Step 7  Transparency
          Every step appears on the public timeline in plain, non-medical
          language.

  Milestone lifecycle:  LOCKED -> ELIGIBLE -> APPROVED -> SETTLED
                        (the last three happen back-to-back, in one sweep)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The system has three layers plus a database:

   +--------------------+      HTTPS / JSON      +----------------------+
   |  Web app (React)   |  <------------------>  |  API server (Express)|
   |  Vite + TypeScript |                        |  Node + TypeScript   |
   +---------+----------+                        +----------+-----------+
             |                                              |
             | wallet signs transactions                    | SQL (Drizzle ORM)
             v                                              v
   +--------------------+                        +----------------------+
   | Solana program     |                        |  PostgreSQL          |
   | (Anchor, Rust)     |                        |  cases, campaigns,   |
   | escrow vault, USDC |                        |  donations, etc.     |
   +--------------------+                        +----------------------+

  Frontend
    React 18, TypeScript, Vite, React Router. Solana wallet adapter for Phantom
    and Solflare. Font Awesome icons. Fully mobile-responsive with a slide-out
    navigation drawer.

  Backend
    Express on Node.js with TypeScript. Drizzle ORM on PostgreSQL. Multer for
    document uploads. AES-256-GCM encryption for sensitive case data and
    uploaded documents. HMAC signing for magic links. Hospital emails are sent
    through SMTP (nodemailer) when configured; in development the link is
    logged instead. A Solana relayer signs escrow actions with a server-only
    platform key. Per-IP rate limiting in production.

  Blockchain
    A Solana program written in Rust with the Anchor framework. It holds USDC in
    a per-campaign escrow vault and releases it only to the registered provider
    wallet.

  Database
    PostgreSQL, run locally with Docker Compose.

  Hosting (live demo: https://rarediseasecf.vercel.app)
    Vercel. The web app is served as static files; the Express API runs as a
    single Vercel Function (api/index.ts -> server/vercel.ts). The database is
    Neon Postgres, encrypted uploads go to a private Vercel Blob store
    (server/storage/fileStore.ts; local disk when not on Vercel), and a daily
    Vercel Cron calls /api/cron/sweep to retry milestone payouts and release
    expired campaigns.

  Networks (setting: SOLANA_CLUSTER)
    There is no simulated mode. The app runs on Solana Devnet (test USDC,
    no real value) or Solana Mainnet (real USDC) - switching is a matter of
    changing the cluster and USDC mint. On either network the server creates
    campaigns on approval, verifies donations, settles milestones, and
    releases expired campaigns to the hospital.

6.4 Backend modules (server/)
-----------------------------
  cases/          Case intake: validation, encrypted storage, document upload
                  with file-type sniffing (PDF/PNG/JPG, max 5 MB, 2 to 8
                  files), optional campaign photo (PNG/JPG, EXIF/GPS and
                  other metadata stripped), 5-letter case IDs, audit events.
  hospitals/      Hospital whitelist, personal-email blacklist, magic-link
                  issue and verify, email delivery, the verification service,
                  approve and reject reason codes, payout method validation
                  (USDC wallet live; fiat returns "coming soon").
  campaigns/      Publishing campaigns, public-safe serializer (explicit
                  allowlist of fields), seeding the four milestones.
  donations/      Records donations, updates amount raised, unlocks eligible
                  milestones, and serves the leaderboard.
  milestones/     Milestone state machine and tranche math: each tranche is the
                  difference between thresholds, with over-settle and
                  double-settle protection.
  escrow/         EscrowService: on-chain custody through the relayer
                  (register campaign, verify donation, settle, release).
  chain/          Solana relayer: creates campaigns on hospital approval,
                  verifies donation transactions, signs milestone settles and
                  expiry releases with the server-only platform key.
  settlement/     Records settlement toward the institution.
  transparency/   Builds the public timeline, updates, and leaderboard,
                  exposing only whitelisted event types.
  reviewer/, auth/  Wallet message-sign login for reviewers and donors.
  security/       AES-256-GCM encryption, case-access cookies, timing-safe
                  secret comparison.
  middleware/     Origin checks, rate limiting, consistent error handling.
  utils/          Case ID generation and small shared helpers.
  db/             Drizzle schema and migrations.

6.5 Database tables (PostgreSQL)
--------------------------------
  cases                  Private case records (sensitive fields encrypted),
                         plus a reference to the optional campaign photo.
  case_documents         Uploaded supporting files (stored off-chain).
  case_access_sessions   Patient's private status access.
  case_audit_events      Full event history, which feeds the transparency
                         timeline.
  hospital_whitelist     Approved hospitals and their email domains.
  magic_link_tokens      Hashed, single-use verification tokens.
  case_review_decisions  Approval and rejection decisions with reasons.
  campaign_drafts        Campaigns with the locked funding gap, amount raised,
                         and publication status.
  campaign_milestones    25/50/75/100 milestones and their status.
  donations              Every donation (amount, method, anonymous flag).
  settlements            Payout records with amount and transaction signature.
  reviewer_registry, provider_registry, reviewer_sessions
                         Allowlisted reviewers, providers, and sessions.

6.6 Solana program (onchain/programs/rarediseasecf)
---------------------------------------------------
  Accounts
    PlatformConfig   Platform admin and the accepted USDC mint.
    VerifierRegistry Approved verifiers.
    ProviderRegistry Approved care providers.
    Campaign         Opaque ID, status, locked funding gap, amount raised,
                     provider wallet, expiry, milestone bitmask.
                     No patient data.
    Vault            A USDC token account controlled by the program, one per
                     campaign.

  Instructions
    initialize_platform        Set up the platform once.
    upsert_verifier /
    upsert_provider            Register verifiers and providers.
    create_campaign /
    approve_campaign           Create a campaign and lock its funding gap.
    donate_usdc                Donor sends USDC into the campaign vault.
    settle_milestone           Release one reached milestone tranche to the
                               provider. Anyone can call it; the threshold
                               is checked and the destination is fixed.
    confirm_fulfillment        Provider confirms treatment was delivered.
    release_provider_payment   Release the exact full gap to the provider,
                               once only.
    release_expired_funds      After the campaign ends, send everything left
                               in the vault to the provider. Anyone can call
                               it; the destination is fixed.

  Hard rules enforced on-chain
    - Money can only go to the provider wallet registered for that campaign.
    - The payout amount is always the locked gap, the milestone tranche, or
      (after expiry) the remaining vault balance - never an amount chosen by
      the caller.
    - Campaigns last at most 180 days. No donor refunds: at the end, the
      hospital receives whatever was raised, so partial funding still reaches
      the treating hospital.
    - A second settlement of the same payment fails.
    - No health information is stored on-chain.

7. SECURITY AND PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Sensitive case text, uploaded documents, and hospital payout details are
    encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM (tampered files are rejected).
  - Documents are never public. Only the hospital holding a valid magic link
    can download them.
  - The hospital wallet address is checked for a valid Solana format, and
    its first and last characters are shown back for a quick match check.
  - On-chain donations are verified against the blockchain before they
    count; a transaction can only be counted once.
  - The platform signing key lives only on the server, never in the browser.
  - Rate limits on submissions, verification links, donations, and admin.
  - The legacy reviewer API switches off in production.
  - The campaign photo is the only public upload. Metadata (GPS, camera,
    author) is stripped, and it is served only after the campaign is
    published.
  - A public Privacy Policy (/privacy) explains data handling and security.
  - Magic links are HMAC-signed, expire within 24 hours, work once, and are
    bound to one case and one email. Only a hash of the token is stored.
  - Personal email domains cannot act as a hospital.
  - Admin actions need a secret header, checked with a timing-safe comparison.
  - Uploads are limited by size, count, and verified file type (the actual
    file bytes are checked, not just the file extension).
  - Public APIs use an explicit allowlist of fields, so internal IDs, bank
    details, wallets, and medical text cannot leak by accident.
  - The frontend holds no private keys; only public network settings.
  - Automated tests cover funding-gap math, milestone rules, double-settle and
    over-settle protection, on-chain donation verification, network
    configuration, the email blacklist, the hospital whitelist, settlement
    references, and expiry handling.

8. HONEST STATUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Real in this build (nothing is simulated)
    - Full web app, API, and PostgreSQL persistence
    - Server-side validation and funding-gap calculation
    - Encryption, magic-link security, and access control
    - Milestone state machine with settlement safeguards
    - Solana escrow program (Rust/Anchor) and wallet integration
    - Donations, milestone payouts, and expiry payouts as real USDC
      transactions on Solana Devnet or Mainnet

  Development convenience
    - Without SMTP configured (development only), the hospital verify link
      is logged to the server console instead of emailed
    - Demo institution (Devnet only): "Himalaya Children's Hospital (Demo)"
      (billing@himalaya-demo.org) is whitelisted for the labelled demo
      campaign. Links addressed to it are never emailed, and it is
      deactivated automatically on Mainnet.

  Not automated
    - Medical document authenticity checks (the hospital verifies)

  Coming soon (shown in the app as "coming soon")
    - Fiat (bank transfer) payouts for hospitals
    - Card / fiat donations for donors
    - USDC-to-fiat off-ramp via Circle / MoonPay

  Not a certified healthcare system. On Devnet, use synthetic data and test
  wallets only.

9. WALKTHROUGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    1. Submit a case at /submit, including the patient's public name,
       condition and treatment and the required public-display consent.
    2. Open the hospital verification link (from the email, or the API log
       during local development). Review, pick USDC payout, approve.
    3. The campaign goes live on /campaigns with its verified badge.
    4. Donate at least 25% of the goal: the 25% tranche is released to the
       hospital wallet by a permissionless instruction the moment the
       threshold is crossed, and shows "Sent" with a View on Solana link in
       the Milestone escrow section.
    5. Open the transparency page: the donation and released milestone appear,
       with no medical documents shown.
